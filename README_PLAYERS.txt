BARTENDER 3D: SKYLINE LOUNGE
============================

Run Bartender3D.exe. No installation needed (Windows 10/11, 64-bit).

You run the bar of a rooftop lounge high above a city at night. Guests sit
down, order a cocktail and you make it the real way: measure with a jigger,
shake in a Boston shaker, strain, stir, muddle, add ice and garnish.

MODES
  Career       Nights from 20:00 to 02:00. Earn money and tips, unlock new
               recipes every night and buy upgrades for your bar.
  Free mixing  No guests, no clock. Put a drink on the gold tasting mat to
               find out what you made and how good it is.

CONTROLS
  Mouse          look around
  WASD           walk behind the bar
  Left click     pick up / put down / use (ice scoop, garnish, cap, strainer)
  Hold right     pour (aim at the glass), shake, stir, muddle
  Shift + right  pour slowly (mouse wheel fine-tunes the tilt)
  E              shaker in hand: cap -> strainer -> open
  R              flip the jigger (60/30, 50/25, 40/20, 30/15, 20/10 ml)
  H              hide / show hints and the recipe card
  Q              put the item back
  X              empty what you hold
  Tab            recipe book
  F              next order
  Esc            pause / settings

MEASURING
  Click a jigger and it goes into your left hand. Take a bottle and hold the
  right button: it pours into the jigger and stops at the brim. Click a tin or
  glass to tip the jigger in. The espresso machine pulls a 30 ml shot.

TIP
  The recipe card on the right ticks off every step as you work.

Your progress is saved automatically.
